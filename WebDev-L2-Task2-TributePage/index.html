Dr. A.P.J. Abdul Kalam

1931 – 2015

Biography

Dr. Avul Pakir Jainulabdeen Abdul Kalam was born on 15 October 1931 in Rameswaram, Tamil Nadu. Coming from a humble family, he worked hard from a young age and believed that education was the key to success. His determination and curiosity helped him overcome financial difficulties and inspired millions of young people across India.

After graduating in Aerospace Engineering from the Madras Institute of Technology, he joined the Defence Research and Development Organisation (DRDO) and later became an important part of the Indian Space Research Organisation (ISRO). His leadership in developing India's satellite launch vehicles and missile programs earned him the title "Missile Man of India."

In 2002, Dr. Kalam became the 11th President of India. Unlike many leaders, he remained closely connected with students and believed that young minds could transform the nation. He travelled across the country delivering motivational speeches and encouraging innovation, creativity, and scientific thinking.

Even after completing his presidential term, Dr. Kalam continued teaching and inspiring people until his last breath. He passed away on 27 July 2015 while delivering a lecture to students. His life remains a symbol of dedication, humility, patriotism, and lifelong learning.
